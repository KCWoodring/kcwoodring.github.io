About Me

When I was twelve, MomoCon’s Artist Alley swallowed me into its twisting mazes for the first time. It contained who I could be as a budding artist, nerd, and storyteller. Now, I provide spaces like that for others. To do that, I create logistically sound, structured environments. These environments make a widely applicable experience personal, memorable, and magical. They breed an atmosphere of creativity and connection.

I’m open to event and community coordination roles. My goal is to offer teens and young adults creative, supportive learning experiences.
